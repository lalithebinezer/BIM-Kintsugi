BIM VIEWER PRO -- HELP & GUIDE
BIM VIEWER PRO 4D / 5D / 6D

Enterprise 3D/4D/5D/6D digital twin platform. Load IFC/FRAG models, inspect elements, simulate 4D construction phasing, estimate 5D project costs, audit 6D carbon lifecycle, and coordinate BCF issues all in the browser.

QUICK START -- 7 CORE STEPS
01
Load a Model
Click Load Sample in the Files panel (Key 1) or upload your own .ifc or .frag file from the bottom dock. The model loads with full element classification and twin metadata.
02
Inspect Elements & Properties
Double-click any 3D element to open the Properties panel (Key 5). View IFC type, name, storey, category, 4D task dates, material volumes, and live 5D unit cost fields.
03
Navigate, ViewCube & Tactical 2D Radar
Orbit by dragging, Zoom with scroll, Pan with right-click. Click faces on the 3D ViewCube to snap views. Use the Tactical 2D Radar (bottom-right) for click-to-teleport, floor plan slicing, and True North compass snap.
04
4D Phasing, Appearance Profiles & Delay Impact
Press Activate 4D in the top ribbon or click the 4D icon on the bottom dock. Drag the timeline scrubber to simulate the build sequence with Synchro-grade Appearance Profiles (Install, Remove, Temporary, Maintain), active appearance growth cutting plane animation, and toggle Variance Mode (V) for comprehensive schedule delay ($SV) & cost variance ($CV) impact analysis.
05
5D Cost Control & ANSI/EIA 748-B EVM Suite
Click the green $ or yellow EVM icon on the bottom dock or inside the 4D timeline. Access multi-model EAC forecasts (Typical, Atypical, Composite, Weighted), time-based Earned Schedule ($SV(t)$, $SPI(t)$), the APM Bulls-Eye Quadrant matrix, and 1-click exports for BOQ, CPR Format 1 (WBS), and CPR Format 5 (VAR).
06
Clash Matrix, BCF Coordination & Carbon LCA
Launch the Clash Matrix, BCF Issue Tracker, or 6D Embodied Carbon directly from the bottom dock. Run automated interference checks, capture viewpoints, and export buildingSMART BCF/JSON reports.
07
Executive Twin Report & Vector Print
Press Ctrl+P or click Executive Report in the Omnibar to open the boardroom executive dashboard with live 4D EVM S-Curve, 5D cost breakdown, 3D clash matrix, and 6D carbon LCA formatted for instant vector PDF printing.
SPECIALIZED TOOLS & ENTERPRISE SUITES
5D Project Cost Control & BOQ Suite
Accessible via the bottom dock $ icon. Features cumulative budget tracking, live 4D/5D EVM S-Curve (Planned Value vs Earned Value), category cost bars, multi-currency conversion (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, INR, CAD, AUD), and Export BOQ spreadsheet.
ANSI/EIA 748-B & APM EVM Control Suite
Accessible via the bottom dock EVM icon. Multi-method EAC forecasting (Typical, Atypical, Composite, Weighted), Lipke time-based Earned Schedule ($SV(t)$, $SPI(t)$, $EAC(t)$), APM Bulls-Eye Quadrant matrix, ANSI 748-B Tier 1 & Tier 2 threshold alarms, and exports for CPR Format 1 (WBS Ledger) and CPR Format 5 (VAR Report).
Tactical 2D Radar & Floor Slicer
Floating HUD on the bottom-right projecting the building footprint. Supports click-to-teleport, camera orientation tracking, True North compass snap, automated storey slicing, and live clash reticle display.
4D Phasing, Appearance Profiles & Delay Engine
Full 4D construction simulator driven by Schedule4DEngine, AppearanceProfileEngine, and DelayImpactEngine. Features Synchro-grade 4D action profiles (Install, Remove, Temporary, Maintain), active appearance growth cutting plane animation, milestone pills, Schedule Variance ($SV), Cost Variance ($CV), Critical Path delay metrics, MSPDI XML / Primavera P6 XER file import, and WebM video recording.
Transparent Ground Grid & Theme Sync
Subtle 0.15 alpha transparent ground reference plane eliminating artificial floor shadows while preserving spatial depth. Automatically synchronizes grid line colors with Obsidian Dark, Light Studio, Zen Minimalist, Cyberpunk, and Blueprint themes. Toggle anytime via Shift+G or the Quick View tools ribbon.
Multi-Discipline Clash Matrix
Real 3D mesh intersection and clearance tolerance audit. Filter by discipline pair (Architecture, Structure, MEP) with 1-click BCF issue promotion, isolated ghosting, and viewpoint capture.
6D Embodied Carbon & LCA Heatmap
ICE v3.0 embodied carbon database engine. Renders a 5-band 3D carbon intensity heatmap (Grade A to E) directly onto building elements with material substitution simulation and sustainability report export.
Magic Dock & Apple Magnifier
Centralized bottom toolbar serving as the single source of truth for all tools. Fluid Apple-style icon magnification, mouse-wheel & touch drag scrolling, and elevated global floating tooltips.
Model Explosion Analysis
Smoothly disassemble building storeys, structural slabs, and MEP assemblies along their centroids using the Explosion Slider.
Precision 3D Dimensions
Snap-to-vertex point-to-point measurement, angle, area, and bounding box volume with live on-screen laser callouts.
Executive Twin Report (Print & PDF)
1-Click generation of an executive construction report modal. Synthesizes 4D SPI, 5D EVM S-Curve, category trade breakdown, 3D clash matrix, and 6D carbon LCA into a boardroom-ready vector print layout with JSON export.
PWA & Offline Jobsite Mode
Installable Progressive Web App with custom Service Worker caching WebAssembly loaders and core assets for reliable offline 3D model navigation on construction site iPads and tablets.
Synthesized Web Audio Soundscape
Native client-side Web Audio synthesizer providing realistic spatial audio feedback for UI clicks, laser measurement snaps, 3D pins, and camera triggers. Includes master volume slider and mute controls.
Cinematic Keyframe Tour & Model Diff
Capture camera keyframe waypoints with smooth bezier interpolation and 360° drone orbits. Run 3D revision comparisons highlighting added (Green), removed (Red), and modified (Amber) elements.
GeoBIM GIS & 3D Satellite Basemap Suite
Accessible via G or the bottom dock globe icon. Georeference your building with WGS84 coordinates, UTM zones, 3D aerial satellite terrain, NOAA solar position alignment, and municipal GeoJSON cadastral boundaries & utility networks.

CAMERA & NAVIGATION MODES
Orbit Mode
Default mode. Left-click drag to orbit around a pivot point. Scroll to zoom. Right-click drag to pan. Best for inspecting models from all angles.
Activate: Key 4 Navigation Mode Orbit or Bottom Dock
First Person Mode
Walk through the building as if you were inside it. Use W A S D to move, mouse to look around. Automatic step elevation handling.
Activate: Key 6 Camera First Person
Top Plan Mode
2D orthographic view locked top-down. Ideal for reviewing floor plans, measuring distances, and checking room layouts without perspective distortion.
Activate: Quick View TOP button
Tactical 2D Radar
Real-time dynamic architectural minimap. Provides elevation auto-tracking, floor slicing, True North orientation snap, and clash marker reticles.
Located at bottom-right viewport corner
Camera Zoom & Dolly Speed Control
Adjust camera zoom speed from 0.1x (micro-precision MEP inspection) to 5.0x (campus & GIS basemap). Use hotkeys [ / ] or - / +, click the canvas HUD stepper, or pick presets in the Scene tab. Touchscreens feature 1-tap Zoom In (+) and Zoom Out (-) buttons.

TIPS & BEST PRACTICES
Use 3D Pins to record field defects during site inspections and download them as a BCF/JSON report for Revit and Solibri.
Use Isolate or Ghosting X-Ray to focus on a single element type without clutter from surrounding slabs and walls.
Use Solar Sun Sliders in Key 4 Scene Settings to evaluate daylighting and seasonal shading conditions.
In 4D mode, set playback to 10x for a fast overview, then slow to 1x to inspect specific construction phases.
Issues created in the sidebar BCF Topic form appear instantly in the header BCF 2.1 Issue Tracker drawer.
Click Board to open the Presentation Sheet drawer, or run Export 4K Presentation Board from Ctrl+K to download a PNG.
Press ? at any time to reopen this guide. Your 4D/5D data and pin annotations are saved locally in the browser cache.
