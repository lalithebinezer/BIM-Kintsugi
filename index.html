BIM VIEWER PRO -- HELP & GUIDE
BIM VIEWER PRO 4D / 5D

Real-time 3D/4D/5D construction twin viewer. Load IFC models, inspect elements, simulate construction timelines, and estimate project costs all in the browser.

QUICK START -- 6 CORE STEPS
01
Load a Model
Click Load Sample in the Files panel (Key 1) or upload your own .ifc or .frag file. The model loads with full element classification and twin data.
02
Inspect Elements & Properties
Double-click any 3D element to open the Properties panel (Key 5). See IFC type, name, storey, category, 4D task dates, and live 5D cost fields.
03
Navigate & 3D ViewCube
Orbit by dragging, Zoom with scroll, Pan with right-click. Click any face on the 3D ViewCube (top-right) to snap to standard elevations or switch to First Person (6).
04
Simulate 4D Construction
Press ACTIVATE 4D in the header. Drag the timeline scrubber to simulate the build sequence. Use speed pills 1x 2x 5x 10x to control playback rate.
05
Estimate 5D Project Cost
Select an element, open 5 INSPECT panel, enter Unit Cost and Quantity. The calculated cost and project grand total update live across all elements.
06
3D Pins, Field Issues & BCF Export
Switch to 7 TOOLS -> Measure / Pins. Drop pins directly on building surfaces, record defect/safety notes with auto-snapshots, and export buildingSMART BCF/JSON reports.
SPECIALIZED TOOLS & ANALYSIS
3D Pin Annotations & Field Issues
Drop 3D pins directly onto model surfaces. Categorize into Inspect, Defect, Safety, RFI, Sign-off. Features auto-viewport snapshots, element tagging, category filtering, and BCF/JSON export.
Solar Sun Study & Dynamic Shadows
Adjust Sun Azimuth (0°-360°) and Sun Elevation (0°-90°) in Key 4 SCENE panel to simulate solar shading and cast dynamic PCF shadows across the BIM geometry.
Model Explosion Analysis
Drag the Explosion Slider in Key 7 TOOLS panel to smoothly disassemble building storeys, structural slabs, and MEP assemblies along their centroids.
X-Ray & Element Ghosting
Click X-Ray in the pin inspector to turn surrounding geometry semi-transparent (20% opacity) while highlighting the tagged IFC element in full color.
Tape Measure
Enable in Key 7 TOOLS tab. Click two points in the 3D viewport to measure real-world distance. Results appear as 3D laser annotations on the model.
4D Timeline Simulation
Activate 4D mode from the header. Elements appear, progress, and complete according to their scheduled dates. Use speed pills 1x to 10x.
5D Cost Estimator
Select any element in the INSPECT panel. Set Unit Cost and Quantity. The calculated cost and running project grand total update live.
Interactive 3D ViewCube
Snap camera to Top, Front, Right, Isometric, or corner views with smooth animation. Cardinal compass ring rotates in sync with scene orientation.
Theme & Post-Processing Engine
Select from 9 architectural themes in the header. Customize Bloom, Vignette, Chromatic Aberration, and GLSL Shader FX in Key 4 SCENE panel.

CAMERA MODES
Orbit Mode
Default mode. Left-click drag to orbit around a pivot point. Scroll to zoom. Right-click drag to pan. Best for inspecting models from all angles.
Activate: Key 4 Navigation Mode Orbit
First Person Mode
Walk through the building as if you were inside it. Use W A S D to move, mouse to look around. Stair-climbing is simulated on elevation changes.
Activate: Key 6 Camera First Person
Top Plan Mode
2D orthographic view locked top-down. Ideal for reviewing floor plans, measuring distances, and checking room layouts without perspective distortion.
Activate: Quick View TOP button
TIPS & BEST PRACTICES
Use 3D Pins to record field defects during site inspections and download them as a BCF/JSON report for Revit and Solibri.
Use Isolate or Ghosting X-Ray to focus on a single element type without clutter from surrounding slabs and walls.
Use Solar Sun Sliders in Key 4 Scene Settings to evaluate daylighting and seasonal shading conditions.
In 4D mode, set playback to 10x for a fast overview, then slow to 1x to inspect specific construction phases.
Press ? at any time to reopen this guide. Your 4D/5D data and pin annotations are saved locally in the browser cache.
